# RitualMac — App Store Listing

## Tagline

**" Rituals that stick."**

---

## Description

RitualMac transforms daily intention-setting into a sacred, unhurried practice.

Start your morning with guided breathwork and a single intention. Check in at evening to reflect on what you embodied. Over weeks, watch patterns emerge between what you intend and how you live.

This is not a productivity app. It's not about streaks or gamification. It's about showing up — with honesty, with presence, with care.

**Features:**
- Morning intention setting with breathing circle
- Evening check-in ritual
- Habit stacking powered by AI
- Accountability partners
- Shared ritual tracking with your partner or household
- macOS menu bar quick access
- Warm, distraction-free dark interface
